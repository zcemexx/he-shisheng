RESEARCH / 02

Image guidance & positioning

Exploring more precise minimally invasive approaches

Research in preoperative localization, image guidance and puncture planning aims to support minimally invasive spine procedures.

01 / OVERVIEW

Ultrasound and preoperative localization

A 2018 technical study reviewed 12 patients (six in each group) and assessed ultrasound volume navigation for puncture in endoscopic discectomy. A 2016 study examined preoperative localization, learning difficulty and fluoroscopy exposure.

02 / PERSPECTIVE

Evidence boundaries

These studies support research into localization and guidance; they do not establish evidence for robot-assisted UBD comparisons.
