RESEARCH / 03

AI & spine imaging

From image segmentation to planning research

Deep learning is used to explore segmentation and 3D reconstruction of lumbosacral structures, including nerves and bone.

01 / OVERVIEW

Automatic segmentation

A 2019 study used a 3D U-Net and 50 CT cases to explore automatic segmentation of lumbosacral nerves and bones; 32, 8 and 10 cases were allocated to training, validation and testing, respectively. A 2020 retrospective study combined 3D reconstruction with simulated foraminoplasty to investigate L5/S1 surgical difficulty.

02 / PERSPECTIVE

From models to clinical practice

These studies explore imaging and planning applications; they do not establish prevention of nerve injury or automated optimal-path generation.
