RESEARCH / 04

Bone repair & targeted materials

Connecting biomaterials and orthopaedic research

Collaborative work in polymer materials explores bone-targeted delivery, tumor imaging and materials for bone regeneration.

01 / OVERVIEW

Targeted delivery and regeneration

A 2021 study explored imaging and drug delivery using bone-targeting polymer vesicles in a rabbit bone tumor model. A 2025 study evaluated miRNA delivery in a mouse bone-metastasis model. Mineralized enzyme materials were also studied in a rat cranial-defect model.

02 / PERSPECTIVE

Preclinical research

These are cell or animal studies. Their findings cannot be directly interpreted as clinical outcomes in spinal fusion or treatment of bone metastasis.
