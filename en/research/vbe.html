RESEARCH / 01

Spinal endoscopy

From instrument design to clinical exploration

This work explores instrument design and lumbar fusion applications in single-portal, bichannel spinal endoscopy.

01 / OVERVIEW

The system and its surgical use

The V-shape bichannel system is compatible with water and air media. A 2023 technical report and preliminary clinical study examined its use in minimally invasive transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion. A 2024 retrospective comparison of 90 patients found differences in some early measures, with no significant difference in satisfaction or fusion rates.

02 / PERSPECTIVE

Research in progress

The selected literature includes technical reports and retrospective studies. Preliminary results alone do not establish comparative efficacy or safety.
