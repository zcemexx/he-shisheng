V-Shape Bichannel Dual-Media Spinal Endoscopy (VBE / UBD)

From original instrument architecture to clinical translation in complex minimally invasive spine surgery

Addressing the coaxial instrument interference and single-medium visibility constraints of conventional uniportal endoscopes, our team pioneered the V-shape bichannel dual-media spinal endoscopy system—establishing an integrated clinical paradigm spanning proprietary instrument design, standardized surgical approaches, and dedicated monographs.

01 / TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Bichannel Decoupling & Water/Air Dual-Media Synergy

The VBE/UBD platform integrates independent optical and working channels within a single minimally invasive incision, restoring multi-angle instrument maneuverability. Its seamless intraoperative switching between saline irrigation and air media combines clear underwater hemostatic decompression with efficient dry-field bone grafting and fusion. Built on this platform, we established standardized procedures including UBD-TLIF and transpedicular anterior dural decompression (UBD-TADD), documented in our Chinese (Science Press, 2021) and English (Springer, 2023) monographs.

02 / CLINICAL TRANSLATION

Evidence-Based Validation & Global Adoption

Comparative clinical cohorts (evaluating VBE-TLIF against conventional MIS-TLIF) demonstrate equivalent long-term fusion rates and patient satisfaction alongside reduced soft-tissue trauma and improved early postoperative recovery metrics. Translated into certified clinical devices and adopted across domestic and international centers, the system is currently undergoing multicenter prospective trials.

02Digital Navigation & Robotic-Assisted PositioningHigh-precision spatial registration and standardized minimally invasive access

Digital Navigation & Robotic-Assisted Spine Surgery

High-precision spatial registration and standardized minimally invasive workflows

To overcome obscured anatomical landmarks and steep learning curves in severe degenerative deformities and revision spine procedures, we integrate miniature optical/electromagnetic navigation and surgical robotics with spinal endoscopy—transforming complex interventions into plannable, reproducible workflows.

01 / SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Dual-Mode Endoscopic Visualization & Real-Time 3D Tracking

Conventional percutaneous targeting relies heavily on surgeon experience and repeated fluoroscopy. By coupling miniature surgical navigation systems with VBE/UBD endoscopy via intraoperative 3D image registration, our workflow provides real-time instrument trajectory tracking and boundary alerts around critical neurovascular structures, enabling sub-millimeter targeting accuracy.

02 / CLINICAL IMPACT

Reduced Radiation Exposure & Accelerated Learning Curves

Clinical evaluations confirm that navigation- and robotic-assisted guidance maintains high decompression adequacy and pedicle screw accuracy while substantially reducing intraoperative fluoroscopy exposure, puncture adjustments, and neurovascular complication risks—significantly shortening the training curve for complex endoscopic procedures.

03AI & 3D Spine ImagingAutomated nerve root segmentation, anatomical assessment and preoperative planning

Artificial Intelligence & 3D Spine Imaging

Automated nerve root segmentation and quantitative preoperative planning

Addressing the indistinct soft-tissue boundaries of lumbosacral nerve roots on clinical MRI/CT and the lack of objective preoperative difficulty metrics, we develop deep learning algorithms for automated 3D anatomical reconstruction and personalized surgical path planning.

01 / ALGORITHMIC INNOVATION

Semi-Supervised & Few-Shot 3D Nerve Root Segmentation

To overcome the scarcity of voxel-level annotated medical datasets, our team developed semi-supervised and few-shot generative adversarial architectures (Semi-cGAN / Fewshot-GAN). These models achieve high-precision automated 3D segmentation and reconstruction of L4–S1 nerve roots, intervertebral discs, and bony corridors, delineating nerve compression and anatomical variations prior to surgery.

02 / CLINICAL DECISION SUPPORT

Preoperative Difficulty Scoring & Trajectory Planning

Leveraging geometric parameters extracted from 3D reconstructions—including iliac crest height, foraminal stenosis, and transverse process morphology—our machine learning models objectively predict procedural difficulty and compute optimal cannula trajectories for challenging L5/S1 percutaneous endoscopic discectomy (PETD).

04Bone Regeneration & Targeted BiomaterialsBiomimetic osteogenic scaffolds and theranostic nanocarriers for spinal metastases

Bone Regeneration & Targeted Biomaterials

Biomimetic osteogenic scaffolds and theranostic nanocarriers for spinal metastases

Focusing on post-fusion bone defect regeneration and targeted therapy for malignant bone tumors and spinal metastases, our interdisciplinary research develops bio-inspired mineralizing scaffolds and bone-targeted polymeric nanocarriers.

01 / BONE TISSUE ENGINEERING

Enzyme-Inspired Biomimetic Mineralizing Scaffolds

Inspired by natural bone biomineralization, we engineer composite hydrogels and porous scaffolds functionalized with alkaline phosphatase (ALP) and biomimetic mineral nanoparticles. These constructs promote localized hydroxyapatite nucleation, osteogenic differentiation, and early vascularization to accelerate spinal interbody fusion and critical-sized bone defect repair.

02 / TARGETED ONCOLOGY

Bone-Seeking Polymer Vesicles for Theranostics

To overcome poor drug accumulation and progressive osteolysis in spinal metastases, we synthesize polymeric vesicles modified with bone-affinity ligands (such as bisphosphonates and aspartate oligopeptides). These nanocarriers selectively deliver chemotherapeutics and therapeutic nucleic acids to osteolytic lesions, integrating diagnostic imaging, tumor suppression, and osteoprotection.

05Spinal Oncology & Clinical Outcome ModelingPrognostic nomograms, risk stratification and clinical evidence synthesis

Spinal Oncology & Clinical Outcome Modeling

Prognostic risk stratification and evidence-based clinical decision tools

Addressing the clinical heterogeneity of primary and metastatic spinal tumors, we leverage large-scale clinical cohorts and statistical modeling to develop individualized prognostic tools that guide surgical and multidisciplinary treatment planning.

01 / PROGNOSTIC MODELING

Nomograms & Web-Based Risk Calculators

For patients with spinal malignancies such as solitary plasmacytoma of bone, our team integrates demographic, anatomical, pathological, and therapeutic variables to construct and validate multivariable prognostic nomograms and interactive web calculators—enabling individualized prediction of overall and cancer-specific survival.

02 / EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS

Clinical Outcomes in Degenerative Spine & Minimally Invasive Care

Drawing upon over three decades of clinical experience encompassing more than 10,000 spine procedures, we conduct systematic outcome analyses across degenerative spinal disorders, interventional spinal pain management, and enhanced recovery after surgery (ERAS) protocols to establish evidence-based clinical guidelines.
